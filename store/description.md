Housing Editor browses compatible HouseDecor rows through wxl-db2, places
installed M2 decor locally, and supports selection, move, rotate, scale,
free-build camera, and experimental client-local terrain tools.

Install wxl-db2 first. For the complete catalog and official housing UI,
[download the Official Housing Content Pack](https://github.com/sogyboi/wxl-housing/releases/latest), then extract every
`wxl-housing-content-<version>-*.zip` asset into the WoW `Data` folder while WoW is closed.
The final path must be `Data/Patch-Housing.MPQ/...`. The extension remains usable
without the content pack but falls back to safe placeholders.

Press Insert or use the draggable building launcher to open the editor.
